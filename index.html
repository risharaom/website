Why we started

We noticed who wasn’t in the room.

We noticed that many students weren’t interested in programming or machine learning because they couldn’t see how these skills could make a difference. In Summer 2025, we started Code4Cause to make ML education more engaging through impactful projects that help students explore real-world problems and see the benefits for their communities.
