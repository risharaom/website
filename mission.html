Our mission

A path into computer science, for more girls.

Code4Cause focuses on getting girls into computer science and technology: building technical skills, confidence, and a path toward CS careers.

We started by helping middle schoolers apply data science to a real problem in the Bay Area. Our focus grew to girls in tech, a network of chapters across countries, and work supporting women’s access to digital wallets in Afghanistan.
